# Orientation

## Objectives
Understand the Academy's learning path, community standards, assessment model and responsible use principles.

## Core lesson
The Academy is designed as a progression from digital literacy to practical Web3 knowledge. Learn the concept first, practise safely, then use the assessment to demonstrate understanding. Practice quizzes are optional; chapter assessments are the progression gates and the final examination is the graduation gate.

A strong learner separates facts from assumptions. When a lesson discusses a network, wallet, payment system or application, verify the current documentation before acting. Never share a private key, seed phrase, password or authentication code with another person.

## Community behaviour
Ask precise questions, explain what you tried, cite reliable sources, and help other learners without shaming beginners. Moderation exists to protect learning quality and member safety, not to suppress disagreement.

## Self-check
Can you explain the difference between learning about an asset and being advised to buy it? Can you describe why credentials and recovery secrets must remain private?
