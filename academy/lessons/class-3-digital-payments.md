# Class 3 — Digital Payments

## Objectives
Understand payment rails, settlement, authorization, fraud controls and safe digital-payment behaviour.

## Payment flow
A digital payment normally involves a payer, payment instrument or account, merchant, intermediary network and receiving institution. Authorization answers whether a transaction is allowed; settlement is the later process that finalizes movement of funds between institutions.

Card payments, bank transfers and instant-payment systems use different rails and operating rules. A transaction identifier, timestamp and amount are useful evidence when resolving a dispute. A screenshot alone is not proof that funds settled.

## Safety
Confirm the recipient before approving a transfer. Treat unexpected requests for one-time passwords, remote access or "verification payments" as suspicious. Never install an unknown APK to receive a refund or unlock a payment.

## Digital assets
Blockchain-based payments differ from reversible bank rails. A transaction can be technically valid yet sent to the wrong address. Network selection, address verification and small test transfers can reduce operational risk.
