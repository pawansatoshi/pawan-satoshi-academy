# Class 11 — DeFi & Wallet Security

## Objectives
Understand decentralized finance primitives and the security model of self-custody.

## DeFi primitives
Lending markets, decentralized exchanges, liquidity pools and staking systems replace or automate parts of traditional financial workflows with smart contracts. Users interact directly with contracts and accept technical and economic risks that a conventional intermediary might otherwise absorb.

A liquidity provider may face impermanent loss, smart-contract bugs and changing market conditions. A borrower can face liquidation. A protocol can have governance, oracle, bridge or upgrade risks. High yield is not a risk-free feature.

## Wallet security
A seed phrase or private key is the root of control. Use hardware or well-maintained wallets where appropriate, back up recovery material offline, and verify every transaction and signature. Avoid blind signing and unknown browser extensions.

Use allowance-management tools carefully and revoke unnecessary permissions. Test unfamiliar applications with small amounts and maintain a separate experimental wallet.
