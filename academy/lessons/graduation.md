# Graduation — Responsible Digital Builder

## Synthesis
Graduation is not a claim of professional certification. It demonstrates that a learner completed the Academy curriculum and passed its required assessment threshold.

A responsible digital builder combines technical knowledge with operational discipline: verify sources, protect credentials, test changes, review permissions, document decisions and understand the limits of each system.

## Final preparation
Review the concepts rather than memorizing answer positions. Revisit any topic where explanations were unclear, use practice quizzes to identify weak areas, and treat the final examination as a demonstration of understanding.

## Capstone questions
Can you explain an internet request from browser to server? Can you outline a security response to a leaked credential? Can you compare a bank transfer with an on-chain transaction? Can you explain why a smart-contract signature is an authorization event? Can you describe the risks of DeFi and stablecoins without relying on price predictions?
